SudokuSudoku is a free Sudoku game in the browser. These terms cover playing, the optional account, the contact form, and ads. What happens to information is in the privacy policy.

Playing

You can play without signing in and without paying. A puzzle is generated in your browser when you start. The daily is the same Not Easy grid for a UTC date, seeded from that date. Without an account, progress, settings, your stats, and the skill ladder stay in local storage on the device.

Optional account

An account is optional. The game still plays if you never sign in. It lets a streak, stats, settings, skill progress, and an unfinished puzzle come back after the browser clears storage, or on another device.

Sign-in is your email and a link or a 6-digit code. There is no password, and no name is stored. The code and the link expire after 15 minutes and each works once. The server stores a hash of them, not the link or the code.

If you sign in, the server keeps your email and a copy of the game data: stats, daily completions, streaks, settings, skill progress, and the puzzle in progress. The session is a cookie page scripts cannot read. It is sent only over HTTPS, not to other sites, and it lasts about 180 days. Sign-in uses Cloudflare Turnstile, the same spam check as the contact form.

Delete my account, in Stats or Settings, deletes the email and the saved game data on the server. The copy on the device stays until you clear site data. I do not sell email addresses or game data.

Children

The puzzles are fine for children. The account asks for an email, so it is not for anyone under 13. I do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13. If a child signed in, delete the account in Stats or Settings, or ask me through the contact form.
