Other places to play and read

Sites, books, forums, and tools that are not part of SudokuSudoku. This is not a ranking. I left off follower counts, because those go stale. If a link here is dead, or you know a good one I missed, use the contact form.

Places to play

Sudoku.com
A big free site, Easy through Expert, with a daily.

Web Sudoku
Old, plain, and still a pile of free puzzles.

SudokuWiki
Andrew Stuart's strategy pages. This is where I go when a technique page of mine feels thin.

Sudoku9x9
A solver that shows the step, not just the filled grid.

Daily Sudoku
A daily puzzle and a forum that has been around a long time.

Puzzle Baron
A large set, from very easy to genuinely mean.

Technique writeups

Sudoku.com rules
Rules first, then the usual techniques up through fish.

Learn-Sudoku
Short lessons, basic moves and then the harder ones.

SudokuOnline tips
Another set of worked examples, beginner through advanced.

Mastering Sudoku
Tips and technique notes in one place.

Conceptis techniques
The technique list from a publisher that also prints the puzzles.

Sudopedia
A glossary. Useful when two sites use different names for the same move.

Forums

The New Sudoku Players' Forum
The long-running forum. Advanced techniques get argued properly here.

r/sudoku
People post stuck boards and, usually, a real explanation.

r/puzzles
Wider than Sudoku, and Sudoku still shows up.

Daily Sudoku forums
Puzzle talk attached to the daily site above.

Video and posts

Cracking the Cryptic
Simon Anthony and Mark Goodliffe solving, mostly variants, on camera. Their account on X is @crypticcracking.

BremSter Puzzles
Shorter solve videos, and the puzzles are worth keeping.

Sudoku.com on Instagram
Daily puzzles from the Sudoku.com account.

Sudoku.com on X
The same publisher, posting puzzles.

Sudoku.com on Facebook
Another Sudoku.com feed.

Daily Sudoku on Facebook
The daily site's page.

Apps

Sudoku.com
The Easybrain app. I still do the New York Times puzzle most mornings, but this is the other one people mean when they say "the app."
iOS | Android

Brainium Sudoku
Themes, undo, and a few levels, on Android.
Android

New York Times Games
Sudoku next to the crossword and Wordle. Easy, Medium, and Hard. Medium and Hard overlap more than the names suggest.
iOS | Android

SudokuPad
Sven Neumann's app, which is what Cracking the Cryptic solves on.
iOS | Web | Steam

Competitions

World Puzzle Federation
Runs the World Sudoku Championship.

WSPC 2025
The 2025 championships, held in Eger, Hungary.

US Puzzle and Sudoku Championships
The US national events.

Logic Masters India
Indian championships, and a lot of online tests.

Variants

Samurai Sudoku
Overlapping grids, one solution, logic only.

Killer Sudoku on Sudoku.com
Cages with sums.

Daily Killer Sudoku
One killer a day.

f-puzzles
A setter tool. A lot of variant puzzles are built here before they show up on YouTube.

Print

Nikoli
The Japanese publisher that put Sudoku in magazines. They still make puzzles by hand.

Penny Publications
Puzzle magazines, including Sudoku, in North America.

Puzzler
UK Sudoku magazines, mild through fiendish.

The New York Times prints a daily Sudoku and keeps a large set in the Games app. The Times of London put Sudoku on its puzzle page on November 12, 2004, which is a big part of why the rest of us know it.

Solvers and setters

HoDoKu
A free Java program that generates, solves, and highlights the step. I learned a lot from it.

SudoCue
A Windows generator and solver. Old, and still strong.

SudokuPad on the web
Same app as above. Import a puzzle, or make one.

Sudoku Coach
A long technique list with pictures of each step.

Artisanal Sudoku
James Sinclair's hand-set variant puzzles.

Conceptis
A publisher's site, classic Sudoku and variants.

Sudoku Station
A small index of forums and groups, if this page missed one.
