Frequently Asked Questions

How do I save my puzzle progress?

Your puzzle progress is automatically saved in your browser's local storage. As long as you use the same browser and device, you can return to your in-progress puzzles at any time.

Can I print Sudoku puzzles?

Currently, our puzzles are designed for online play only. We're working on adding a print-friendly feature in the future!

How often do you add new puzzles?

Our puzzle database contains thousands of unique puzzles across all difficulty levels. Each time you start a new game, you'll receive a different puzzle.

Do you offer Sudoku apps?

Our website is mobile-responsive and works great on phones and tablets. For dedicated mobile apps, check out our Links page which features the best Sudoku apps for iOS and Android.

Can I submit my own puzzles?

We love hearing from puzzle creators! Please reach out via the contact method above if you'd like to discuss puzzle submissions or collaborations.
