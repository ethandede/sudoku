This policy says what SudokuSudoku does with information when you play, when you choose to save progress with an email, when Google measures or advertises, and when you send a message.

Playing does not need an account

You can play without signing in. Puzzle progress, settings, your own stats, and the skill ladder are stored in local storage on your device. They are not cookies. Anonymous play sends none of that to an account, because there is no account until you ask for one. Clearing the site's data in your browser deletes the copy on the device. Anonymous community stats, described below, are separate, and only when sharing is on.

Optional account

Sign-in is optional. It exists so a streak, stats, settings, and a puzzle you have not finished can come back after the browser clears storage, or when you use another device. The game still plays if you never sign in.

If you sign in, the server stores your email address and a copy of the game data on this site: stats, daily completions, streaks, settings, skill progress, and the puzzle in progress. It does not store a name or a password. Sign-in is a link and a 6-digit code sent to that email. The code and the link expire after 15 minutes and each works once. The server stores a hash of them, not the link or the code itself.

A sign-in request also stores a hash of your IP address for a short time, so the form can limit how many emails it sends. The address itself is not stored. Those rows are deleted when they expire, and when you delete the account.

The form uses Cloudflare Turnstile, the same spam check as the contact form. That check sends a token, and Cloudflare may receive connection details such as your IP address, under Cloudflare's privacy policy.

While you are signed in, the browser sends that game data to your account when you finish a puzzle, when you leave the page, and when you open the site. If the network is down, the device keeps the data and tries again later. Signing in merges the copy on the device with the saved copy. A daily completion is kept from either side. The longer streak is kept. Played and won counts keep the higher number for each level, so the same win is not counted twice. A best time keeps the faster one. An unfinished puzzle keeps whichever copy was saved more recently.

The session is a cookie. Page scripts cannot read it. It is only sent over HTTPS, and it is not sent with requests that other sites make. It lasts about 180 days and refreshes when you come back. It is used only to recognize the account.

Delete my account, in Stats or Settings, deletes the email and the saved game data from the server. The copy on the device stays until you clear site data. I do not sell email addresses or game data, and I do not use them to advertise.

Anonymous play stats

The public stats page shows how long puzzles take and how often they are solved, given up, failed, or left unfinished. That comes from plays on this site. Sharing is on unless you turn off "Share anonymous play stats" in Settings.

A shared play sends the puzzle level, whether it is the daily, the UTC date for a daily, a hash of the puzzle seed or of the givens, the outcome, the active seconds on the timer (pause is not counted), mistakes, how many hints you used and the hardest technique among them, whether you used notes, and a device class taken from the width of the window: phone, tablet, or desktop. It does not send the puzzle itself, the solution, your name, your IP address, or the browser's user agent string. The server does not store those, and it does not build a fingerprint.

The browser keeps a random id in local storage under the key sudoku-anon-id. It is not a cookie. It exists so the same puzzle is not counted twice for the same browser. Turn "Share anonymous play stats" off and that id is deleted, along with any stats waiting to be sent. Clearing site data for sudokusudoku.com deletes it too. A new id, if you turn sharing back on, does not reconnect to the old one.

That id is storage on your device, and the stats are not required to play, so it is not strictly necessary. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland the game does not create the id and does not send stats until Google's consent message reports that analytics storage is granted. That is the same choice as Analytics on the other pages. This page does not load that message. Use Privacy choices on another page if you want to change it. Outside those countries the Settings toggle is the control, and it starts on.

A puzzle you close and resume is the same play. Replacing it with a new game, or coming back to find the saved game gone, marks it abandoned. Hitting the four-mistake limit does not, until you leave that game, because undo continues it. A start with no finish after 24 hours is marked abandoned on the server. Groups with fewer than 20 finished plays are not shown.

Contact form

If you use the contact form, the name, email address, and message you enter are emailed to me so I can read them and reply. A hidden field is there for spam bots. If that field is filled in, the message is discarded.

The form uses Cloudflare Turnstile. Turnstile checks that a person submitted it. That check sends a token, and Cloudflare may receive connection details such as your IP address, under Cloudflare's privacy policy. I do not publish your email address on the site.

If mail delivery is not configured, the form says so and does not send anything.

Ad settings are at Google's Ad Settings. You can also install the Analytics opt-out add-on.

Visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland get a consent message from Google's certified consent management platform. Consent Mode defaults on those visits are denied for ad storage, ad user data, ad personalization, and analytics storage until you choose. In other regions those defaults are granted. Privacy choices, in the footer of the other pages, reopens Google's consent message. It is not on this page. On a page that does not load the ad script, such as the links page, the control tells you to open the home page and use it there.

What this site does not do

The site is served globally through Cloudflare. It is not hosted in one country. Your saved game stays on the device unless you use the optional account, in which case the email and game data described above are stored in Cloudflare's D1 database. The anonymous play stats above are separate gameplay fields in that database, and only when sharing is on. Messages you send through the contact form, and sign-in emails, are delivered as email and may be processed by Cloudflare and by whatever mail provider sends or receives them.

The links page points at other people's sites. Their privacy practices are their own.

Children

The puzzles are fine for children. The optional account asks for an email address, so it is not meant for anyone under 13. I do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13. If a child signed in or sent a message through the contact form, delete the account from Stats or Settings, or use the contact form to ask me to delete it.

Asking about your information

The personal information I receive is the email on an optional account, and whatever you put in the contact form. You can delete an account yourself in Stats or Settings. You can also ask to see, correct, or delete it through the contact form. There is no marketing list to opt out of.
