This policy says what SudokuSudoku does with information when you play, when Google measures or advertises, and when you send a message.

Playing does not need an account

You do not register. Puzzle progress, settings, stats, and the skill ladder are stored in local storage on your device. They are not sent to a server, and they are not cookies. Clearing the site's data in your browser deletes them. Using a different browser or device starts fresh.

Contact form

If you use the contact form, the name, email address, and message you enter are emailed to me so I can read them and reply. A hidden field is there for spam bots. If that field is filled in, the message is discarded.

The form uses Cloudflare Turnstile. Turnstile checks that a person submitted it. That check sends a token, and Cloudflare may receive connection details such as your IP address, under Cloudflare's privacy policy. I do not publish your email address on the site.

If mail delivery is not configured, the form says so and does not send anything.

Google Analytics and AdSense

This page does not load Google Analytics, the AdSense script, or the Funding Choices consent message. The game and the other pages load Google Analytics (measurement ID G-TFNKPH6DNY) and the Google AdSense script (publisher ca-pub-9345046210950956). Google may use cookies to measure visits and to serve and measure ads. How Google uses data from sites that use its services is described at Google's partner sites policy. Ad settings are at Google's Ad Settings. You can also install the Analytics opt-out add-on.

Visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland get a consent message from Google's certified consent management platform. Consent Mode defaults on those visits are denied for ad storage, ad user data, ad personalization, and analytics storage until you choose. In other regions those defaults are granted. A visit is not, by itself, consent to cookies.

Privacy choices, in the footer of the other pages, reopens Google's consent message. It is not on this page. On a page that does not load the ad script, such as the links page, the control tells you to open the home page and use it there.

What this site does not do

The site is served globally through Cloudflare. It is not hosted in one country, and puzzle data is not uploaded to a server in the United States or anywhere else. Messages you send through the contact form are delivered as email and may be processed by Cloudflare and by whatever mail provider receives them.

The links page points at other people's sites. Their privacy practices are their own.

Children

The puzzles are fine for children. I do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13. If a child sent a message through the contact form, use that same form to ask me to delete it.

Asking about your information

The personal information I receive is whatever you put in the contact form. You can ask to see it, correct it, or delete it by writing again through the contact form. There is no marketing list to opt out of.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it.
