How to play

Fill every row, column, and 3×3 box with the digits 1 to 9, each one once.

Entering numbers

  • Normal puts a digit in the selected square. Candidate pencils in small notes instead.
  • On a selected empty square, tap one of the small digit spots to toggle that note.
  • Undo takes back your last move. Erase clears the selected square.
  • Auto Candidate Mode notes every digit that still fits in every empty square.

Hints

The lightbulb points at the next step and names the technique. Tap Show why for the reasoning, then Apply or Place it to make the move. Each hint links to a page on that technique.

Mistakes

With Check guesses as you go on in Settings, a wrong digit counts as an error, and the fourth ends the game. Turn it off to play with no limit.

Keyboard

  • 1 to 9 enter a digit
  • N switches between Normal and Candidate
  • Arrow keys move between squares
  • Backspace erases
  • Enter places a hinted digit
